## Overview

The Product Management feature allows you to maintain a complete inventory catalog with automatic stock tracking, category organization, and barcode support for quick lookups during sales.

## Product Information

Each product in your inventory contains:

- **Product Name**: Descriptive name for easy identification
- **Barcode**: Optional barcode for scanner integration (unique per user)
- **Category**: Organize products into custom categories
- **Purchase Price**: Cost price paid to suppliers
- **Selling Price**: Price charged to customers
- **Current Stock**: Real-time inventory level
- **Creation Date**: When the product was added to the system

## How to Add Products

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Products">
    From the main menu, click on **Products** to access your inventory
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add Product">
    Click the **Add Product** button (Admin only)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Product Details">
    Fill in the product information:
    - Product name (required)
    - Barcode (optional)
    - Category
    - Purchase price
    - Selling price
    - Initial stock quantity
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save Product">
    Click **Save** to add the product to your catalog
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Only Admin users can add, edit, or delete products. Sellers can view products to create sales.
</Note>

## Managing Products

### Viewing Products

The product list displays all your inventory with:
- Product name and current stock level
- Category and pricing information
- Quick actions for editing or deleting
- Pagination (7 products per page)

### Filtering by Category

Use the category filter dropdown to view only products in a specific category. This is helpful when you have a large catalog organized by product types.

### Sorting Options

Sort your products by:
- **Name** (alphabetical)
- **Stock level** (low to high or high to low)
- **Price**
- **Date added**

### Editing Products

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Product">
    Click the **Edit** button next to the product you want to modify
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update Information">
    Change any product details including name, prices, or category
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save Changes">
    Click **Save** to update the product
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Editing a product's purchase price does not affect existing sales records. Only new sales will use the updated price.
</Warning>

### Deleting Products

To remove a product from your inventory:

1. Click the **Delete** button next to the product
2. Confirm the deletion

<Warning>
  You cannot delete products that have been sold. The system will show an error message indicating related sales must be deleted first.
</Warning>

## Barcode Scanning

Inventario supports barcode scanning for fast product lookup during sales:

### Setting Up Barcodes

1. When adding or editing a product, enter the barcode number in the **Barcode** field
2. Barcodes are unique per user/admin account
3. You can use standard barcode formats (EAN-13, UPC, etc.)

### Using Barcodes in Sales

During the sales process:
- Connect a USB barcode scanner to your device
- Focus on the barcode field in the sales form
- Scan the product barcode
- The system automatically finds and adds the product to the sale

<Tip>
  If you don't have a barcode scanner, you can manually type the barcode number in the search field.
</Tip>

## Stock Management

### Automatic Stock Updates

Inventario automatically adjusts stock levels when:

- **Sales are created**: Stock decreases by the quantity sold
- **Sales are edited**: Stock adjusts based on the difference
- **Sales are deleted**: Stock is restored
- **Purchases are recorded**: Stock increases by the quantity purchased
- **Purchases are deleted**: Stock decreases accordingly
- **Returns are processed**: Stock is restored for returned items

### Low Stock Alerts

The system monitors stock levels and alerts you when:
- Products reach zero stock
- Products fall below the low stock threshold (default: 5 units)

<Note>
  Low stock warnings appear immediately after completing a sale that brings inventory below the threshold.
</Note>

## Tips for Success

- **Start Simple**: Begin with your most popular products and expand your catalog gradually
- **Consistent Naming**: Use clear, consistent names so products are easy to find
- **Regular Audits**: Periodically verify physical stock matches system records
- **Category Strategy**: Don't create too many categories - keep it manageable
- **Price Strategy**: Ensure your selling price covers costs and desired profit margin

## Troubleshooting

### Product Not Found by Barcode

- Verify the barcode is entered correctly in the product details
- Check that the barcode scanner is working properly
- Ensure the barcode belongs to a product owned by your account

### Cannot Delete Product

If you see an error when trying to delete a product, it means the product has sales associated with it. You have two options:
1. Keep the product in your catalog (recommended)
2. Delete all associated sales first (not recommended)

### Stock Count Doesn't Match

If your physical stock doesn't match the system:
1. Review recent sales and purchases
2. Check for any deleted or edited transactions
3. Manually adjust the stock level by editing the product

<Tip>
  For significant stock adjustments, document the reason in your records for future reference.
</Tip>
